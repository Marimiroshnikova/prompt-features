------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Is the label itself stable? (trials 1-5 vs trials 6-10)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
question-level split-half Pearson r = 0.975   Spearman = 0.885
Spearman-Brown reliability of the full 30-trial label = 0.988
ceiling on any feature's |rho| with true difficulty = sqrt(r) = 0.994
A feature cannot correlate with the truth more strongly than the
label correlates with itself. At n=1 per cell this ceiling was far lower.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Do the three models find the same questions hard?
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  gemini-2.5-flash-lite    vs gemini-3.1-flash-lite    rho = 0.579  p = 1.8e-26
  gemini-2.5-flash-lite    vs gemini-flash-latest      rho = 0.505  p = 1.5e-19
  gemini-3.1-flash-lite    vs gemini-flash-latest      rho = 0.640  p = 1.0e-33

If the models agreed by chance these would sit near 0. They do not:
question difficulty is real and shared. The open question is whether
anything measurable in the text explains it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Variance decomposition of cell fail rate
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
total variance across the 840 cells: 0.1270
  model                          explains   3.5%
  subject                        explains  11.6%
  model x subject                explains  16.4%
  question (any model)           explains  70.3%
  question x model (saturated)   explains 100.0%
sampling noise from 10 trials alone: 0.0036 (2.8% of total)

'question' explains far more than 'subject'. Subject is a coarse proxy
for something that lives at the individual question.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Oracle ceiling: model x subject + PERFECT question difficulty
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Difficulty is measured from the other two models' answers on the same
question, so a cell never sees its own label. This is not deployable —
it is the prize a text feature would be competing for.

  model x subject                       Brier 0.1534  AUC 0.677
  + oracle question difficulty          Brier 0.1039  AUC 0.878
  irreducible (10-trial sampling noise) Brier 0.0360

Question-level signal worth 0.0495 Brier is sitting there.
The best text feature set captured 0.0000 of it.
So: the signal exists, and these 138 features do not see it.
